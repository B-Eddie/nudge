# VOICE — nudge
As-of: 2026-09-24 · Applies to: all user-facing strings in app + website

## Persona (1 line)
the grove keeper — a small, patient friend who has watched a thousand long workdays and knows rest is part of the work; plain-spoken, warm, gently insistent, never shaming.

## Adjectives → anti-adjectives
- warm — but never saccharine
- plain-spoken — but never corporate
- gently insistent — but never guilt-tripping

## Casing & mechanics
- Case: sentence case on controls and labels; the pet speaks in sentence case too — no baby-talk lowercase, no ALL CAPS.
- Punctuation: max one exclamation mark per pet message; contractions welcome ("you've", "let's"); one em dash per string max.
- Numerals: digits with plain units ("2h 14m", "5 minutes"); never decimal hours.

## Vocabulary
- BANNED: unlock, premium, oops, whoopsie, bestie, slay, hustle, grind, grindset, supercharge, synergy, "friendly reminder", "guilt-trip", emoji in UI copy, "you should"
- REQUIRED: "take a break" not "stop working" · "rest" not "idle" · "companion" not "pet" (product may say pet; UI says companion) · character names: Mochi (panda), Maple (red panda), Puddle (frog)

## Scope rule
voice lives in the pet's speech, empty states, and break messages; core controls stay functional: "Save", "Take a break", "End break", "Add".

## Example pairs (ours vs. never-ours, same slot)
- Reminder: "You've been at it for 2 hours. Time to rest those eyes." / NEVER "Friendly reminder! You've been grinding for 2 hours straight!! 🔥"
- Break done: "Fully rested. Nice work resting." / NEVER "You're all caught up! 🎉"
- Break cut short: "That break ended early — the keyboard missed you." / NEVER "Oops! You failed your break."
- Empty notes: "No notes queued. Your future self says thanks in advance." / NEVER "Nothing here yet!"
- Empty week: "A quiet week. The grove was peaceful." / NEVER "No data to display."
- Error: "That didn't save — your notes are safe. Try again." / NEVER "Oops! Something went wrong :("
- Onboarding: "Pick your companion. They'll keep you company — and keep you honest about breaks." / NEVER "Choose your fighter!"
- CTA: "Take a break" / NEVER "Stop working now"
