# Pixel crab artwork credits

The crab and activity poses are adapted from **Clawd emotes** by **xixicc186**. The source collection is MIT licensed.

- Source pack: https://github.com/xixicc186/clawd-emotes-skill
- License: MIT; see `LICENSE.txt` for the full text.
- Adaptations: the source poses are fitted to Nudge's 64×64 frame. The productivity scene uses the source reading emote and the social scene uses its photo emote. Nudge's idle and greeting poses use the source crab anatomy and palette.

The source pack describes its artwork as original and inspired by the separate `clawd-on-desk` project; no upstream `clawd-on-desk` assets are included here.
