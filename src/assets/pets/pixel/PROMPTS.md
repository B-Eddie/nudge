# Pixel companion sprite sheets

Generated using the built-in ImageGen tool. Each transparent atlas contains sixteen expressions and activity poses. Original PNGs are preserved; the app samples atlas cells onto a 60-pixel canvas with nearest-neighbor rendering and a shared baseline.

## crab

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production pixel-art desktop-pet SPRITE ATLAS. Create ONE transparent PNG sprite sheet, exact FOUR columns by FOUR rows, equally sized square cells, 16 separate full-body poses of THE SAME character. Style: beautiful hand-crafted 64x64 indie-game pixel art, expressive cozy creature, sophisticated 16-color palette, deliberate sharp square pixel clusters, warm highlight and cool shadow ramps, dark colored one-pixel silhouette, rich small details, beautiful character design readable at small size. NO smooth drawing, NO line illustration, NO 3D rendering, NO anti-aliased edges, NO gradient, NO text, NO labels, NO grid lines, NO scenery. All 16 cells have genuine transparent background. Each character centered, equal scale, all feet on an IDENTICAL baseline at 85% cell height, complete ears/tail/claws and props inside cell with 10% padding, identical character design and colors throughout. Render each sprite as if created at 64x64 and enlarged by nearest-neighbor, never a downsampled realistic illustration. The grid must fill a square image exactly, no outer margins or uneven spacing.
Cell order, left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: (1) neutral alert idle, (2) same idle body with eyes fully closed for blink, (3) joyful pleased smile with relaxed paws, (4) delighted eyes-closed grin with raised paws.
Row 2: (5) curious head tilt, (6) surprised while being picked up, paws dangling, (7) tired eyes and wide yawning mouth, (8) curled asleep with closed eyes.
Row 3: (9) standing walking pose left foot forward, (10) identical walking pose right foot forward, (11) stretching arms upward, (12) grooming face with one paw/claw.
Row 4: (13) concentrating at a small laptop, (14) reading an open cream book, (15) listening with small headphones and pleased eyes, (16) playing with a small game controller.
Make actual facial expressions differ clearly. Most sprites face forward with a slight three-quarter orientation. Do not change species between cells. No ground shadows, no duplicate sprites, no missing cells. Subject: a coral-orange crab with two black expressive eyestalks, rounded articulated pincers, peach belly and six tiny legs.
