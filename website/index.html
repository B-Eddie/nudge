HOW IT WORKS

Work well. Rest well.
Keep going.

Built for the rhythm of an actual workday, not another dashboard to manage.

01

Settle in

Your pet sits quietly in a screen corner. Nudge counts time by app category on your Mac, without an account or cloud sync.

02

Take the hint

As the work stretches on, your pet gets tired. Gentle reminders arrive on a schedule you choose.

03

Step away

Take a break and let both of you recharge. Your daily and weekly reports show where your time went.

MADE FOR REAL WORKDAYS

Just enough to help.
Never more than that.

No streak pressure. No crowded productivity cockpit. Just a companion you can make your own.

01

Reminders on your terms

Set the interval, pick a tone, or keep only the timed nudges. Your own notes can become reminders too.

02

Your time, in context

See a simple daily and weekly breakdown by app category, with breaks alongside work time.

03

Private by design

Your activity and preferences stay on your Mac. Pause tracking or clear your activity history when you want.
