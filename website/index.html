why nudge

A companion, not another dashboard

nudge stays out of your way while you work and shows up exactly when you need a push to rest.

Silky pixel animations

Every sprite is predecoded at launch, so state changes — focused, resting, nudging — swap instantly with zero flicker. Your companion reacts to the app you're actually using.

Featherweight footprint

Built on Tauri with a native Rust core — a ~10 MB app that idles at near-zero CPU and a sliver of RAM. It's a companion, not a Chrome tab.

Nudges you control

Set your own work/break rhythm, queue custom reminder notes, and let auto-breaks kick in when you step away or your computer sleeps. Energy recharges only when you actually rest.

how it works

Three steps to healthier focus

1

Adopt your companion

nudge settles into a corner of your screen and quietly tracks what you're focused on — no accounts, all data stays local.

2

Get nudged

The longer you work, the more its energy drains. On your schedule, it pipes up with a reminder — or one of your own queued notes.

3

Rest & recharge

Take the break — your companion naps and recharges with you. Check daily and weekly reports to see where your time really went.

Give your desktop a heartbeat
