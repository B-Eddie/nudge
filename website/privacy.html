Privacy · Updated October 1, 2026

Your day stays
on your Mac.

A small companion should come with a clear explanation of what it can see. Here’s how the Nudge app and this website handle information.

Nudge keeps activity summaries, preferences, and reminder notes locally. The desktop app has no account system, advertising, or analytics service, and does not upload this information to a Nudge server.

What the desktop app reads

  • The name and identifier of the active app, and its category, to adapt the companion’s behavior and summarize time by category.
  • Time since keyboard, mouse-button, or scrolling activity, to recognize idle stretches and avoid interrupting a break. It does not record the keys you type or the content of your work.
  • Pointer position and mouse-button state for petting and dragging, plus window positions and sizes so the companion can perch on a window.
  • Installed app names, identifiers, locations, and category metadata to populate the app-category settings.

Nudge does not take screenshots or read document text, messages, browser history, or page contents.

What it saves

Activity summaries contain time by app category, breaks taken or interrupted, rest duration, and the longest work stretch. Nudge keeps the current session and up to 31 daily activity summaries. It also saves your preferences, custom app categories, and notes waiting for a reminder.

These live in activity.json and settings.json in the app’s local configuration folder. They are ordinary local files and may be included in backups you make of your Mac.

You’re in control

You can change app categories, reminder timing, and launch-at-login behavior in Settings. Quit Nudge to stop its activity monitoring.

To remove saved activity, quit the app and remove activity.json from ~/Library/Application Support/com.eb.nudge/. Removing settings.json also resets preferences, custom categories, and pending notes. The app creates fresh files when you next use it.

About this website

The interactive demo runs in your browser. It cannot see which apps are open on your Mac or read your desktop activity. This site does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account forms, or cookies set by Nudge.

Your selected demo companion and mood are saved in browser session storage for this tab. This preference contains no desktop activity and is cleared when the tab session ends.

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes normal website requests and may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. See Vercel’s privacy notice for its practices.

Downloads and source-code links take you to GitHub, where GitHub’s own privacy practices apply. If you submit a public issue, share only information you are comfortable making public.

Questions about privacy?
